# Aethera (AE2) — Tokenomics

## Overview

AE2 is the native digital asset associated with the Aethera project.

Aethera is focused on building a technical transparency layer for digital assets through a Website-First approach.

The AE2 token is deployed on Ethereum Mainnet.

## Website-First Model

Aethera follows a Website-First architecture.

The project does not require a centralized exchange, decentralized exchange, wallet, or custodial platform to operate its core concept.

The primary focus is the development of a web-based technical information layer for digital assets.
