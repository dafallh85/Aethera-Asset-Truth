# Aethera Technical Principles

## 1. Technical Truth First

Aethera prioritizes verifiable technical information before opinions, marketing claims, or assumptions.

## 2. Facts vs. Claims

Technical facts and project-provided claims should be clearly separated.

## 3. Source Awareness

Important information should be connected to an identifiable source whenever practical.

## 4. Verification Status

Information should have a clear status when applicable:

- Verified
- Confirmed
- Reported
- Unverified
- Not Available
- Under Review

## 5. Transparency

Aethera should communicate limitations clearly rather than presenting incomplete information as certainty.

## 6. Neutrality

Aethera provides technical information and does not represent an endorsement of any digital asset or project.

## 7. Accessibility

Complex blockchain information should be organized so that both technical and non-technical users can understand the essential facts.

## 8. Continuous Improvement

Aethera may evolve its data sources, verification methods, architecture, and tools as the ecosystem develops.
