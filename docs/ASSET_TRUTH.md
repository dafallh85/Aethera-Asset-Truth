# Aethera Asset Truth

## Purpose

Aethera organizes important technical information about digital assets into a clear and accessible asset profile.

The goal is to help users understand an asset before interacting with it.

## Asset Profile

An Aethera asset profile may include:

- Asset name
- Token symbol
- Blockchain network
- Contract address
- Token standard
- Total supply
- Circulating supply
- Decimals
- Contract verification status
- Ownership information
- Mint function
- Burn function
- Transfer restrictions
- Buy tax
- Sell tax
- Proxy status
- Liquidity information
- Token distribution
- Important contract events

## Information Status

Each piece of information should be clearly identified according to its status.

Possible statuses include:

- Verified
- Confirmed
- Reported
- Unverified
- Not Available
- Under Review

## Transparency Principle

Aethera should not present assumptions as facts.

When technical information cannot be independently verified, it should be clearly identified as unverified or unavailable.

## Asset Truth Model

Aethera focuses on three questions:

### 1. What is the asset?

Identify the asset, network, contract, supply, and technical structure.

### 2. How does the asset work?

Explain relevant contract functions, permissions, restrictions, taxes, ownership, and other technical characteristics.

### 3. What should users know before interacting?

Present important technical information that may affect how users understand or interact with the asset.

## Important Notice

Aethera provides technical information and transparency tools.

An Aethera profile is not an endorsement of an asset and does not constitute financial, investment, trading, legal, or professional advice.

**Know the asset before you trust the asset.**
