# Aethera Glossary

## Asset

A digital asset represented on a blockchain.

## Asset Profile

A structured collection of technical information about a digital asset.

## Asset Truth

Aethera's approach to organizing and presenting verifiable technical information about digital assets.

## Blockchain

A distributed network used to record and verify transactions and other digital asset activity.

## Contract Address

The blockchain address that identifies a smart contract.

## ERC-20

A common token standard used for fungible tokens on Ethereum and compatible networks.

## Smart Contract

Blockchain-based code that executes defined functions according to its programmed rules.

## Total Supply

The total number of tokens issued by a token contract.

## Decimals

The number of decimal places used to represent token amounts.

## Contract Verification

The process of making a smart contract's source code publicly available and matching it to deployed contract bytecode through a supported block explorer.

## Ownership

Information describing the address or mechanism controlling administrative permissions of a smart contract.

## Mint

A function or mechanism that can create additional tokens.

## Burn

A mechanism that permanently removes tokens from circulation.

## Proxy Contract

A contract architecture in which calls may be delegated to another implementation contract.

## Liquidity

Assets available in a trading pool or other market mechanism to facilitate transactions.

## Verification Status

A label describing the current level of confidence or confirmation for a piece of information.

## On-Chain Data

Information recorded directly on a blockchain.

## Web3

The ecosystem of decentralized applications, blockchain networks, digital assets, and related technologies.
